# Ruqyah content map — what the 10 videos teach

> Source material for the app. Pulled from the on-screen captions of the 10 TikTok videos and the comment screenshot (Nov 2026).
> **Every item has a label**, so the app never mixes proven Sunnah with teachers' experience.

| Label | Meaning | How the app shows it |
|---|---|---|
| ✅ **SUNNAH** | Comes from an authentic hadith or a direct Quran text | Green badge "From the Sunnah" + hadith reference |
| 🟡 **EXPERIENCE** | Allowed under the general rule *"no harm in ruqyah without shirk"* (Muslim 2200), but **no specific hadith**. The teachers say it comes from their experience. | Amber badge "Teachers' experience" + one-line note |
| ⚪ **SUPPORT** | Good practice that supports healing but is not ruqyah by itself | Grey badge "Helpful habit" |
| ❌ **AVOID** | A claim the app must not present as a promise | Not shown |

> ⚠️ **Hadith numbers below need checking by a scholar before launch** (box in todo.md). They follow the usual Bukhari/Muslim numbering.

**What all 10 agree on** 🔑
- **You are the best person to do your own ruqyah** (videos 2, 4, 5, 8, 9).
- The core is always **Fatiha + Ayat al-Kursi + last verses of Baqarah + the 3 Quls + Prophetic duas**.
- **Blow** into your hands and **wipe** your body / the place of pain.
- **Be consistent**; it's short ("doesn't take five minutes", video 4).
- **Medicine too**: ruqyah doesn't replace a doctor (video 10).

## 2. Core session — Sunnah only (the MVP "Start my ruqyah" flow)

| Step | What to do | Count | Label | Evidence | From video |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Intention**: seek healing from Allah alone | — | ✅ | "Actions are by intentions" (Bukhari 1) | 1, 8, 9 |
| 1 | **Wudu** (preferred, not required) | — | ⚪ | Scholars: wudu is recommended, not a condition | 1 |
| 2 | **A'ūdhu billāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm** + Bismillah | 1 | ✅ | Quran 16:98 | 9 |
| 3 | **Sūrat al-Fātiḥah** | 1 · 3 · 7 (odd) | ✅ | The Prophet ﷺ called it a ruqyah (Abu Sa'id al-Khudri, Bukhari 5749, Muslim 2201) | 3, 4, 7, 9 |
| 4 | **Āyat al-Kursī** (2:255) | 1 | ✅ | Protection hadith (Bukhari 2311) | 3, 4, 5, 8, 10 |
| 5 | **Last 2 verses of al-Baqarah** (2:285–286) | 1 | ✅ | "They will suffice him" (Bukhari 5009, Muslim 807) | 4, 5, 10 |
| 6 | **al-Ikhlāṣ, al-Falaq, an-Nās** | 3 each | ✅ | 'Ā'ishah: he recited them, blew and wiped (Bukhari 5017, 5751) | 1, 3, 4, 8, 10 |
| 7 | **Hand on the pain**: "Bismillāh" ×3, then *A'ūdhu billāhi wa qudratihi min sharri mā ajidu wa uḥādhir* ×7 | 3 + 7 | ✅ | 'Uthmān ibn Abī al-'Āṣ (Muslim 2202) | 3, 9, 10 |
| 8 | **Duas** (see section 3) | as listed | ✅ | — | 4, 10 |
| 9 | **Blow** lightly (dry spit) into palms, **wipe** face and body, starting where you can reach | — | ✅ | 'Ā'ishah (Bukhari 5017) | 1, 7, 8, 9 |

Counting options (from Tim Humble, video 9): **3 times, 7 times, or as many as you like without counting.** abostani (video 4): "however many, as long as it's an odd number." → The app offers **1 · 3 · 7 · Free** on every counter.

## 6. ❌ Not shown in the app as a promise
- "They recited al-Fātiḥah **200 times** and the jinn left" (video 8): a **story**, not evidence. If shown at all, then only as "a teacher's story", never as a rule.
- Any **symptom checklist that "diagnoses" sihr, jinn or evil eye**: not in the videos and not for an app. Reason: fear, wrong self-diagnosis, missed medical problems.
- Embedding or re-uploading the TikTok clips: the videos belong to their creators. Link to the teachers' own channels instead.
